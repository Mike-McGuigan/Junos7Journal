# Dawn over Komiža

Before the harbour had properly woken, Cameron looked east across Komiža as the first light of day rose behind the hills of Vis. With Juno’s 7 lying quietly at anchor and the water almost mirror-calm, it was one of those peaceful Mediterranean mornings that few visitors experience from this viewpoint.

## Did you know?

Komiža was one of the principal Vis locations used as the fictional Greek island of Kalokairi in *Mamma Mia! Here We Go Again*.
