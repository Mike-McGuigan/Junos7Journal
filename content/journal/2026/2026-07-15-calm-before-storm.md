# Calm Before the Storm

As evening settled over Smokvica Vela, the underwater lights beneath Juno’s 7 transformed the sea around the tender into a glowing turquoise pool. Beyond it, the anchorage faded into the deep blue of the Adriatic.

> “A storm is just about to hit us 🥴”

Sophie’s next update explained that the storm looked large on the radar and the crew were moving the yacht to find better shelter. The later AIS track showed only a short repositioning within the same anchorage rather than a move to another bay.

## Later that evening

> “Moving the boat now to find a bit of shelter apparently the storm is looking big on the radar!”
