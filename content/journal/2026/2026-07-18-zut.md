# Žut Harbour

After moving to the sheltered waters of Žut Harbour, protected by the small island of Gušćak, Cameron and Sophie went ashore for dinner at Konoba Vison. Sophie ordered scampi expecting the familiar breaded version served at home, but received whole Adriatic langoustines in tomato sauce instead.

One escaped while she was cracking it open, sending sauce across her white trousers and putting Cameron firmly in the splash zone. The evening ended with an encounter with Strawberry, a free-roaming local donkey.
