# Superyacht Company in Zadar

While moored in Zadar, Juno’s 7 shared the harbour with the striking 52-metre motor-sailer Reposado. Built in Croatia by Tramontana Yachts and delivered in 2024, her twin-masted profile made her stand out even among the harbour’s impressive line-up.
