# An Accident on the Dock

While cleaning Juno’s 7’s windows from the dock in Zadar, deckhand Toni was concentrating on the glass above him and did not realise he had reached a flight of concrete steps. He fell down the steps and partly into the water, fracturing his radius near the elbow joint. His arm was placed in a full cast, with a repeat X-ray planned after a week and an initial recovery estimate of three to four weeks.
