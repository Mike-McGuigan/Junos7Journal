title: A Quiet View in Kablin Bay
location: Kablin Bay, Zverinac Island, Croatia
type: anchorage

While anchored in Kablin Bay, Sophie shared the view aft from Juno’s 7 across clear turquoise water towards the wooded shoreline of Zverinac Island. With an inflatable kayak floating alongside and the bay almost perfectly still, it captured the calm of another sheltered Croatian anchorage.
