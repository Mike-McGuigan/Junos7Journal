After the long passage south from Zadar and across to Italy, Juno’s 7 arrived in Crotone Old Harbour in time for breakfast. It marked the yacht’s first confirmed Italian stop of the season and brought the voyage from the Croatian islands into Calabria on the Ionian coast.

## Did you know?

Crotone began as the ancient Greek colony of Kroton in the eighth century BC. The city is closely associated with Pythagoras, who founded his school there, and nearby Capo Colonna preserves remains of the sanctuary of Hera Lacinia.

## Flavour of Calabria

Calabrian food is known for bold, sun-warmed flavours: chilli, oregano, wild fennel, aubergines, cured pork, cheeses such as pecorino and caciocavallo, and a glass of Cirò from the region’s prized vineyards.
