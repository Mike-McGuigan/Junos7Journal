With the owners back on board and four guests joining them for a few days, Juno’s 7 left Grand Harbour and anchored off Comino. While the yacht remained at anchor, SETE took the guests across to the Blue Lagoon for an excursion. Later, Sophie shared the evening view across the anchorage, with Comino’s limestone cliffs and cave openings catching the last warm light.

## Did you know?

Comino sits between Malta and Gozo and is famous for clear turquoise water. The Blue Lagoon is the best-known swimming spot, while nearby Crystal Lagoon is more enclosed, with cliffs, caves and deeper blue water. Together they make Comino one of Malta?s most recognisable cruising areas.
